Approach

How I work

Five habits behind every release I sign off.

  1. Test where the risk is

    I map where a failure would hurt most, like payments, login and identity checks, and put the deepest coverage there first.

  2. Put quality in the pipeline

    Suites run on every change, so problems show up in minutes instead of during a long manual regression.

  3. Release on evidence

    Go or no-go rests on numbers everyone can see: defect leakage, automation coverage and release readiness.

  4. Use AI, verify everything

    My own Claude skills draft scenarios, frameworks and run reports. Nothing counts until I have checked it against the real product.

  5. Raise the whole team

    I have mentored 10 QA/SDET engineers and work closely with product, engineering and compliance, so quality is shared, not a final checkpoint.
